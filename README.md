# Werewolf AI

This is an AI game were you can play against AI characters. The game is based on the game of Werewolf.

## Rules

The game proceeds in rounds, alternating between Night and Day phases.

**Roles:**
*   **Werewolves:** Secretly know each other. Each night, they choose one player to eliminate.
*   **Villagers:** Don't know who is who. Their goal is to identify and eliminate the Werewolves.
*   **Special Villagers (Common examples):**
    *   **Seer:** Each night, can choose one player to learn their true identity (Werewolf or not).
    *   **Doctor:** Each night, can choose one player to protect from the Werewolves' attack.
*   **Moderator:** A non-player who facilitates the game. in this case, it is the game itself that does the moderation

**Night Phase:**
1.  The Moderator tells all players to close their eyes ("go to sleep").
2.  Werewolves are told to wake up, silently agree on a victim, and go back to sleep.
3.  The Seer is told to wake up, points to a player whose identity they wish to know. The Moderator signals the result (e.g., thumbs up for Werewolf, thumbs down for Villager). The Seer goes back to sleep.
4.  The Doctor is told to wake up, points to a player they wish to protect, and goes back to sleep.
5.  (Other special roles may have actions during the night).

**Day Phase:**
1.  The Moderator tells everyone to wake up.
2.  The Moderator announces who was killed by the Werewolves during the night (unless they were saved by the Doctor). That player reveals their role and is out of the game. If no one was killed, the Moderator can announce that.
3.  All remaining players discuss who they suspect might be a Werewolf.
4.  Players nominate candidates for elimination.
5.  All players vote on who to eliminate (lynch). The player with the most votes is eliminated, reveals their role, and is out of the game. Ties may result in no elimination or a re-vote, depending on house rules.
6.  The game proceeds to the next Night phase.

**Winning:**
*   **Villagers win** if all Werewolves are eliminated.
*   **Werewolves win** if the number of Werewolves is equal to or greater than the number of remaining Villagers.
